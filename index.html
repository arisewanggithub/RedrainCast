Simple sharing between compatible peers

Choose what to share

On Windows, choose a window or display. On Android, approve screen sharing through Android's own consent flow.

Find a nearby receiver

Discover receivers on the LAN, or connect using the receiver's displayed IP address and port.

Make it your session

Use optional output audio and playback statistics. Android also offers microphone audio when explicitly selected and permitted.

RedrainCast connects to compatible RedrainCast peers. It is not a generic Miracast or Google Cast receiver. Performance and available capture/audio features depend on your devices, content and network; no fixed frame rate is guaranteed.

Use only on a trusted network

Incoming casts are accepted automatically. Discovery and casting traffic are not authenticated or encrypted. Anyone who can reach the receiver's listening port may send a cast. Do not expose it to the internet or use it on an untrusted network.
